**Description:**

**Learn any language, one word at a time.**

One Word is a beautifully simple language learning app designed for busy people. Instead of overwhelming you with lessons, we give you ONE carefully curated word every day — with everything you need to truly understand and remember it.

**WHY ONE WORD?**

Most language apps throw hundreds of words at you. Research shows this leads to forgetting 80% within a week. One Word takes the opposite approach: deep learning of one word per day, 365 words per year.

**WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT:**

-- Cultural Context: Understand HOW and WHY native speakers use each word

-- 11 Languages: English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Italian, Turkish, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese
-- CEFR Levels: Words organized from A1 (beginner) to C2 (mastery)
-- Spaced Repetition: Science-based review system for long-term memory
-- Streak System: Build daily learning habits with motivating streaks
-- Beautiful Design: Clean, distraction-free learning experience
-- 100% Offline: Learn anywhere, no internet needed

**365 EXPERTLY CURATED WORDS PER LANGUAGE:**

Each word includes:
- Native pronunciation guide
- Example sentences with translations
- Related words and synonyms
- Cultural usage notes
- Grammar explanations
- Real-world examples from pop culture

**PERFECT FOR:**

- Travelers preparing for a trip

- Busy people who want to learn in just 2 minutes/day

**SUPPORTED LANGUAGES:**
