# Privacy Policy for One Word

**Last updated:** March 8, 2026

## Overview
One Word ("we", "our", "the app") is a language learning application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

## Data We Collect
- **Learning progress:** Words learned, quiz scores, streak data, XP points — stored locally on your device only.
- **Preferences:** Language selections, notification settings, display preferences — stored locally on your device only.
- **No account required:** One Word does not require user registration or login.

## Data We Do NOT Collect
- We do **not** collect personal information (name, email, phone number)
- We do **not** track your location
- We do **not** use analytics or tracking tools
- We do **not** share any data with third parties
- We do **not** use cookies

## AI Chat Feature
When you use the AI practice feature, your conversation is sent to an AI service for generating responses. No conversation data is stored permanently.

## Local Storage
All your learning data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. If you uninstall the app, all data is deleted.

## Children's Privacy
One Word does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app is suitable for all ages.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date.
